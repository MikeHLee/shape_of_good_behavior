\begin{abstract}
As autonomous systems are deployed in complex environments, ensuring behavioral safety becomes critical. Standard Safe Reinforcement Learning methods like Constrained Policy Optimization (CPO) use expectation-based constraints that allow for catastrophic tail events, particularly when deceptive rewards overwhelm soft penalties. In this paper, we introduce a geometric approach to behavioral safety based on Conformal Riemannian Metrics and Sheaf-Geodesic Policy Optimization (SGPO). By defining dangerous states as geometric singularities (``behavioral black holes'') where the conformal factor $\sigma(x) \to \infty$, we render unsafe regions geodesically unreachable by any policy update. \textbf{[WITHDRAWN 2026-07-21]} This abstract previously claimed that in our ``Murky Drone'' and ``Agentic Shortcut'' scenarios PPO and CPO violate safety constraints 100\% of the time while SGPO achieves 0\%. That claim is \emph{refuted}: a 50-seed multi-step re-implementation finds CPO safest (180.7 violations/seed) and SGPO's headline formulation statistically indistinguishable from unconstrained PPO ($p=0.68$). The empirical section must be rewritten before this draft is used. See \texttt{EXPERIMENT\_ISSUES.md} \S8/\S12.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Impact Statement}
